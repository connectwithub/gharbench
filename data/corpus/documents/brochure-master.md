# Kalpana Heights

**By Mrigtrishna Realty Pvt Ltd** | Sector 7, Chitralekha Road, Nayanagar, Maharashtra

_Where imagination comes home._

## The project

Kalpana Heights is a four-tower residential community built in two phases on
Chitralekha Road, in the heart of Nayanagar's fastest-growing residential
corridor.

- **Phase 1 (Towers A and B)** is complete, with its occupancy certificate
  received. Move in now: 2 and 3 BHK residences in Tower A, and 3 and 4 BHK
  residences - including a duplex penthouse - in Tower B.
- **Phase 2 (Towers C and D)** is under construction, with possession declared
  for Q4-2028 under its own RERA registration. Tower C offers compact and
  premium 2 BHK layouts; Tower D offers 3 and 4 BHK residences.

Each phase is separately RERA-registered. Registration numbers, carpet areas,
prices and payment plans are in the phase price sheets - every figure quoted
to you comes from those sheets, not from this brochure.

## Why Nayanagar, why now

Minutes from the metro and the arterial highway, walking distance to a
well-regarded school, and a short drive from the InfoTech Park that anchors
the city's employment - the location works as hard on a Tuesday as it does on
a Sunday. The full landmark list with distances is in the amenity document.

## Life at Kalpana Heights

A dozen curated amenities across the podium and rooftop - clubhouse, gym,
