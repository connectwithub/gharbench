# Kalpana Heights - Specifications and Finishes

Applies to all towers unless a phase is named. Structural and finishing
specifications; areas and prices are in the phase price sheets.

## Structure

- RCC shear-wall construction, seismic zone III compliant design
- External walls: AAC block with double-coat sand-faced plaster
- Lobby-to-lobby floor height 3.05 m (finished ceiling ~2.75 m)

## Living, dining and bedrooms

- Flooring: 800 x 800 double-charged vitrified tiles
- Internal walls: gypsum-finished, low-VOC acrylic emulsion
- Windows: three-track powder-coated aluminium sliders with mosquito mesh

## Kitchen

- Granite platform with single-bowl stainless steel sink
- Ceramic dado up to 600 mm above platform
- Provision for water purifier, chimney and washing machine (utility)

## Bathrooms

- Anti-skid ceramic flooring; full-height glazed wall tiles
- Chromium-plated fittings; concealed plumbing with hot-cold mixers
- Solar-assisted hot water supply in all master bathrooms

## Doors

- Main door: engineered hardwood frame with laminated flush shutter,
  video-door-phone provision
- Internal doors: hardwood frames with painted flush shutters

## Electrical

- Concealed copper wiring with modular switches, MCB and ELCB protection
- AC provision in living room and all bedrooms
- 100% DG power backup for common areas; 1 kVA backup per residence

## Safety

- Fire detection and sprinkler system per NBC norms
- CCTV coverage of entrances, lobbies, podium and basement
- Two high-speed passenger lifts and one service lift per tower

## 4 BHK duplex penthouse (Tower B) additions

- Private terrace with pergola provision
- Internal staircase in Italian marble
- Double-height living room
